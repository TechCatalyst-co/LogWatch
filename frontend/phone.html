Send a log to the big screen

Nothing is read from your phone automatically. You choose exactly what to send, and it only goes to the booth dashboard.

1 · Check your phone's privacy log

Where do I find it?

Android 12+: Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Privacy dashboard. Tap Location, Camera or Microphone to see which apps used it and when.
iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → App Privacy Report (turn it on, it fills in over a few days). The orange/green dot at the top of the screen means mic/camera is in use right now.

2 · Paste any log (advanced)

Copied lines from a router admin page, Windows Event Viewer or a Raspberry Pi? Paste them here, one per line.
